# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers, pledge to make taking part in the tug
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- being kind to other people, and patient with newcomers;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- taking responsibility for our mistakes, apologising to those affected, and
  learning from the experience;
- focusing on what is best for the community as a whole, not just for us as
  individuals.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any
  kind;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing other people's private information, such as a physical or email
  address, without their explicit permission. In a project about reaching
  clusters, that includes cluster addresses, account IDs and credentials
  someone shared by mistake: tell them privately instead;
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in every community space: issues, pull requests,
reviews, discussions and chat about tug. It also applies when someone
officially represents the community in public, such as by posting from an
official account or acting as an appointed representative at an event.

## Our responsibilities

The maintainers are responsible for making clear what acceptable behaviour
is, and for taking fair and appropriate action in response to behaviour they
consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. They may remove,
edit or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues and other
contributions that do not follow this code of conduct, and will explain their
reasons when they do so where that is appropriate.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to
the maintainers through GitHub: open the **…** menu on the issue, pull
request or comment, choose **Report content**, and then
**Report to repository admins**. Only the maintainers see the report. Every
report is reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

For behaviour that isn't tied to something posted here, or that breaks
GitHub's own rules, choose **Report abuse** in the same menu, or use
[GitHub's reporting form](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse),
which goes to GitHub's staff.

The maintainers keep the reporter's identity and the details of a report
private, and do not retaliate against anyone who reports in good faith.

## Enforcement guidelines

The maintainers follow these guidelines in deciding what to do about a
breach of this code of conduct:

1. **Correction.** For inappropriate language or other behaviour considered
   unprofessional or unwelcome: a private, written warning that explains why
   the behaviour was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning.** For a single breach, or a series of actions: a warning with
   consequences for continued behaviour. No interaction with the people
   involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing the code,
   for a set period, in community spaces and on external channels such as
   social media. Breaking these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent
   ban.
3. **Temporary ban.** For a serious breach, including sustained inappropriate
   behaviour: a temporary ban from any interaction or public communication
   with the community for a set period. Breaking these terms may lead to a
   permanent ban.
4. **Permanent ban.** For a pattern of breaches, harassment of an individual,
   or aggression towards or disparagement of groups of people: a permanent ban
   from any public interaction within the community.
